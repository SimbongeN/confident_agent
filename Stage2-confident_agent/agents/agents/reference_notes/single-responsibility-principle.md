# Single Responsibility Principle (SRP)
keywords: SRP, single responsibility, single responsibility principle, one reason to change, SOLID, class responsibilities, separate responsibilities
- A class should never have more than one reason to change.
- Responsibilities should be separated into separate classes.
- Each responsibility is an axis of change.
- A change to one responsibility may impair the class's ability to meet its other responsibilities.
- A class should not be overloaded with multiple responsibilities.
- Tight coupling leads to fragile designs that break in unexpected ways, with cascading and ripple effects in unexpected areas of the source code.
- It is one of the five SOLID design principles for managing dependencies in OO design (Robert C. Martin).
- The Common-Closure Principle is the SRP restated for packages.
